Our little beginning

Coffee with a sense of place.

We opened in 2021 with one espresso machine, a borrowed banana bread recipe, and a big love for this city. Today we work with independent Indian roasters and local farms. Our bread takes the time it takes, our coffee is sourced with care, and there is always room for one more at the table.

What matters to us

Care in the little things.

01

Know your coffee

We partner with independent Indian roasters and take time to bring out the character in every bean, from the first espresso to the last pour-over.

02

Cook with the season

Our kitchen keeps things thoughtful and fresh. We choose ingredients for flavour, make what we can in-house, and let the menu change with the market.

03

Make room for everyone

A quick cup, a birthday brunch, a solo afternoon: we want every visit to feel easy, welcoming, and entirely your own.

Made by people who care

The hands behind your cup.

Little Ember is a team effort: early starts in the kitchen, thoughtful pours at the coffee bar, and warm welcomes at the door. We share ideas, learn from every cup, and take pride in the small details that make a visit feel special.

We hope you will get to know us over a favourite order, a recommendation from the team, or a conversation that lasts a little longer than planned.

From our neighbourhood

A place that grows with Jaipur.

Civil Lines has become part of our story. We love the familiar faces, the new neighbours, and the little moments that turn a café into a meeting place. Every day, we get to share a table with this community.

Whether you have been with us since the first cup or just found us today, we are glad you are here.
