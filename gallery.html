Life at Little Ember

Coffee, crumbs, and the corners we love.

Behind the cups

A little care, every day.

Mornings begin early: the espresso machine warms up, pastries come out of the oven, and the first light reaches the tables. By lunchtime, there is the sound of plates being shared and friends finding each other.

Later, the pace softens again. The cups get refilled, the playlist gets mellow, and the last sun finds its way to our favourite seats. These are the small scenes that make Little Ember feel like ours—and yours.
